# Learn Autonomous Driving with Phoebe

Autonomous driving as a data problem, for data people moving into the field and the leaders who fund the work.
Sixteen 45-minute sessions in two tracks: six leader sessions (no code) and ten practitioner sessions (hands on),
ending in a prove-it-safe bench computed in the browser.

- Running case: Lanehart, a constructed robotaxi pilot fleet in a fictional city. Every fleet number is generated
  by a seeded engine (`assets/av-live.js`) and labelled constructed.
- Real inputs: NHTSA's 2022 crash summary (1.33 deaths per 100 million vehicle miles), RAND's "Driving to Safety"
  method, the SAE J3016 levels chart, NHTSA's Standing General Order page, and the published design of a public
  multi-sensor research dataset.
- The engine runs six self-checks on load; a Python reference reproduces every bench number.

Static site, no build step: open `index.html`. Not investment advice; no real company, fleet or incident appears.
